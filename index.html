📋 Overview

This project builds a complete Discounted Cash Flow model to estimate the intrinsic value of Wingstop Inc. (WING) using publicly available financial data. The model forecasts free cash flows over a multi-year horizon, discounts them at the weighted average cost of capital (WACC), and adds a terminal value — producing an estimated fair value per share.

The analysis examines Wingstop's franchise model, unit economics, and same-store sales growth to ground the assumptions driving the valuation. Sensitivity analysis then tests how the output moves across ranges of growth and discount rate assumptions.

⚙ Methodology
01
Historical Financial Analysis
Parse Wingstop's income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement. Calculate historical FCF margin and growth trends.
02
Revenue & FCF Forecasting
Project revenue growth using unit count expansion and same-store sales assumptions. Drive FCF from operating income minus capex.
03
WACC Estimation
Estimate cost of equity via CAPM using beta, risk-free rate, and equity risk premium. Blend with after-tax cost of debt by capital structure.
04
Terminal Value & Intrinsic Value
Apply Gordon Growth Model for terminal value. Discount all cash flows back to present and divide by diluted shares outstanding.
05
Sensitivity Analysis
Build two-way sensitivity tables showing intrinsic value across a matrix of WACC and terminal growth rate combinations.
